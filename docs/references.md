## Round Eye — Surveyor 3

**Equipment:** Surveyor 3 lander. **Launch vehicle:** Atlas-LV3C Centaur-D.

- [NASA Science — Surveyor 3](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/surveyor-3/): mission overview.
- [NASA Science — Surveyor 3 Stories](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/surveyor-3/stories/): articles about the mission.
- [NSSDC — Surveyor 3 Record](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1967-035A).
- [NSSDC — Surveyor 3 Dataset Catalog](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/displayDataset.action?spacecraftId=1967-035A).

## Twinkle — Apollo 11 Laser Retroreflector

**Equipment:** Laser Ranging Retroreflector (LRRR), a reflector used for laser distance measurements.

- [NSSDC — Apollo 11 Lunar Module / EASEP Record](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1969-059C).
- [NSSDC — Laser Retroreflector Experiment Record](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/experiment/display.action?id=1969-059C-04).
- [NSSDC — PSCM-00011 Dataset Record](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/dataset/display.action?id=PSCM-00011).
- [Apollo 11 Preliminary Science Report — Laser Ranging Section (PDF)](https://ilrs.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/2026/lunar/Apollo11_Preliminary_Sci_Report_LLR.pdf).

## Sharp Ear — Apollo 12 Seismic Experiment

**Equipment:** Passive Seismic Experiment (PSE), the passive seismic experiment deployed by Apollo 12.

- [NSSDC — Apollo 12 Lunar Module / ALSEP Record](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1969-099C).
- [NSSDC — Passive Seismic Experiment Record](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/experiment/display.action?id=1969-099C-03).
- [NSSDC — Apollo 12 PSE Dataset Search](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/dataset/query.action?spacecraftName=Apollo+12+Lunar+Module+%2F+ALSEP&experimentName=Passive+Seismic+Experiment+%28PSE%29).

## Mesh Wheels — Apollo 15 Lunar Rover

**Equipment:** Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV), the rover used during the Apollo 15 mission.

### Overview and Archival Images

- [NSSDC — Apollo 15 Lunar Module / ALSEP Record](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/spacecraft/display.action?id=1971-063C).
- [NSSDC — Apollo Lunar Roving Vehicle](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/lunar/apollo_lrv.html): overview of the rover.
- [NASA — Lunar Roving Vehicle, Apollo 15 Mission](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/lunar-roving-vehicle-apollo-15-mission/): archival photograph of the Apollo 15 rover.
- [LROC — Image and Article 491](https://lroc.im-ldi.com/images/491).

### Reports and Operating Procedures

- [NASA — Apollo 15 Mission Report (PDF)](https://sma.nasa.gov/SignificantIncidents/assets/apollo-15-mission-report.pdf).
- [NASA History — Apollo 15 Mission Summary](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/SP-4029/Apollo_15a_Summary.htm).
- [NASA Technical Reports Server — Document Record 19730010465](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19730010465).
- [NASA — Apollo 15 Final Lunar Surface Procedures (PDF)](https://an.rsl.wustl.edu/apollo/data/A15/resources/A15_SurfaceProc.pdf), dated 9 July 1971: §3.6.1, “Walk-Back Limit”, page 220; personal life support system (PLSS) consumables, pages 218–219.

## Sky Watcher — Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter

**Equipment:** Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO). **Launch vehicle:** Atlas V 401.

### Mission and Instruments

- [NASA Science — LRO](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/lro/): mission overview.
- [NASA Science — About LRO](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/lro/about/): general information.
- [NASA Science — LRO Spacecraft & Instruments](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/lro/spacecraft-and-instruments/): spacecraft and scientific instruments.
- [NASA SVS — Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Gallery](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/gallery/lunar-reconnaissance-orbiter/): visual resources about the mission.

### Impacts and Polar Illumination

- [NASA SVS — March 17, 2013 Lunar Impact Forms a New Crater](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4242/): material about the impact on 17 March 2013 that created a new lunar crater.
- [NASA Science — Shackleton Crater’s Illuminated Rim & Shadowed Interior](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/shackleton-craters-illuminated-rim-shadowed-interior/): Shackleton Crater’s illuminated rim and shadowed interior.
- [NASA Science — Illumination Map of the Moon’s South Pole](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/illumination-map-of-the-moons-south-pole/): illumination map of the Moon’s south pole.

## Using Sources in the Project

- When editing factual pages, choose sources directly relevant to the content and place source links in the footer as required by [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).
- When using archival images, provide the correct attribution and caption for each image; see the [image asset catalog](design/image-assets.md).
- Pages labeled **“Contains game elements”** do not need displayed citations under the current convention.
